# Personal AI Pods and custom agents

Secretary, Coach and Quartermaster extend the built-in roster without replacing existing identities. Private custom specialists have explicit responsibility, effort, requested output-token limits and enabled state. A private AI Pod defines up to six selected specialists and a mission-level reservation budget; Chief synthesizes all members and Critic follows the other specialists. Pod mission enqueue is explicit, never automatically scheduled. All requests are read-only; owner responsibility is data under the fixed safety instructions, not a tool grant. No personal source excerpts, cross-pod memories or social data are included in a Pod mission. The exact team configuration is fingerprinted at enqueue and rechecked before transport and completion; edits or disabled members halt subsequent work. Model selection remains pinned by the configured provider. Export/delete-all cover configuration. UI and isolated memory follow.
