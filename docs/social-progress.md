# Explicit shared progress and cooperative challenges

Members may publish only a UTC day, self-reported minutes and session count to a specific Pod. There is no automatic personal OS import, no private objectives/URLs, and no leaderboard. The service labels all totals as self-reported. Delete a day's projection to revoke it; leaving/removal cascades the member's shared progress and challenge participation. Blocks filter entries and viewer-specific aggregate totals. Pod moderators may create bounded cooperative challenges; members explicitly join or leave, and only published days within the challenge window count. Totals are viewer-specific and may differ with blocks or deletion. No incentive to exceed a daily limit or punish breaks. History is limited to 30 UTC days on reads and pruned on new publication; a scheduled cleanup and UI remain.
